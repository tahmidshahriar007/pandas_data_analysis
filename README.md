# 🚕 NYC Taxi Revenue & Tipping Analysis

> **Executive Summary:** An end-to-end data analysis project using Pandas to clean raw NYC taxi trip records, engineer trip duration/cost metrics, and uncover revenue patterns across boroughs.

---

## 📌 Project Highlights

* **Data Cleaning & Quality Control:** Handled missing location zones, imputed payment methods, and filtered out invalid zero-fare/zero-distance records.
* **Feature Engineering:** Converted text timestamps into date/time objects to derive trip duration in minutes and cost per mile.
* **Key Findings:**
  * **Manhattan** accounts for the vast majority of trip volume (~5,200+ trips) with an average fare of $11.07.
  * **Queens** yields the highest average fare per trip ($24.49), driven by long-distance airport traffic.
  * **Credit card users** tip an average of $2.92 on Yellow Taxi trips, whereas cash payments show $0.00 in digital tip records.
